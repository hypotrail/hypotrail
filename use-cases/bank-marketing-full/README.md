# Bank Marketing (full file, in time order)

**J0 scope:** the analysis contract declares a retrospective comparison. Campaign
order is documented, but complete feature snapshots, client history and the time
each training outcome became available are not. Ordered evaluation alone does not
establish future pre-call performance; dependencies remain explicitly unknown.

Generality case from v0.2b: the same telephone campaigns as [Bank Marketing](../bank-marketing/README.md), with every row instead of the 10 % sample, so that the sealed final test is **the latest fifth of the calls** (a temporal holdout). The `bank-marketing` case (`bank.csv`) is unchanged and stays the v0.2a reference.

## What the case says

`case.yaml` is generated by `build_case.py` from `../bank-marketing/case.yaml`: the same variable dictionary (groups, French labels, availability statements, special values) and scenario facts, plus:

- `order: {by: line, direction: ascending}`, a `documented_fact` (`bank-names.txt:31`: "bank-full.csv with all examples, ordered by date (from May 2008 to November 2010)"). The `month` column agrees: it runs from May to November in 30 consecutive runs. The final-test cut is the last 9,042 lines (`L36171` to `L45212`); 893 of them are `bank.csv` records, already exposed, which stay development rows (`prior_exposed_rows`, below), so the sealed final test holds **8,149 rows** and the development rows are 37,062: the earlier calls plus those 893.
- the verification facts (`data_*`, `observed`) and the prior exposure: the dataset is widely published, and the 4,521 rows of `bank.csv`, on which the Hypotrail tutorial published results, are rows of this file. Their row keys are listed in `data/prior-exposed-rows.txt` (generated by `build_case.py`, exact record match) and declared as `prior_exposed_rows`: the engine never seals them, so no `bank.csv` record can sit in the final test of a Bank full study (review M2, ADR 0006 amendment).
- `scenario_defaults.capacity_share: 0.10` (`scenario_assumption`): the exercise assumes the team can call one client in ten. A real team gives its own figure.

The year is not recorded, so there is still no time column: the development rows are split at random (stratified) inside the earlier period, and only the final test is in the future of the development rows. A model chosen on the earlier calls is tested on later ones, whose conditions may differ.

## Files

- `data/bank-full.csv`, `data/bank-names.txt`: the data and their documentation.
- `data/prior-exposed-rows.txt`: the row keys of the `bank.csv` records in `bank-full.csv` (generated).
- `build_case.py`: generates `case.yaml` (`--check`, `--verify`, `--out DIR`). Regenerate it after a change of `../bank-marketing/case-supplement.yaml` (and of `../bank-marketing/case.yaml`); `--check` fails until then.
- `case.yaml`: the [`hypotrail.case/1`](../../docs/specs/CASE-CONTRACT.md) adapter. Edit `build_case.py`, never the YAML.
