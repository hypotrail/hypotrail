Is the outcome rare?

eda.target_ratecurrent

Class imbalance: a model that always says “no” would be right 88.5 % of the time, so accuracy will mislead. Judge models against this base rate.

Are the codes “-1” and “unknown” one fact or several?

eda.special_valuescurrent

pdays = -1 and poutcome = unknown are the same 2,964 rows: one fact, “never contacted before”. Treat them as one category, not as the number -1.

Also coded unknown: contact 1,059 rows · education 150 · job 30.

Does the result of the previous campaign go with subscribing now?

eda.rate_by:poutcomecurrentsorted by rate · scale 0–70 %

Clients whose previous campaign succeeded subscribed far more often (64.3 % of 103). This describes the development sample; it does not say that a past success causes a subscription.
