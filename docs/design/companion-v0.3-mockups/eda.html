Exploration of the training rows

6 looks at the outcome · each counted
✓All 9,864 development rows are explored; each is later predicted by a model that did not see it. The 2,466 final-test rows are never read here.
Why all of them?

With 5-fold cross-validation there is no separate validation partition: every development row is used to explore, to fit (in four folds) and to measure (in one). That is why these figures are development evidence, not final evidence.

Almost half the sessions never open an account page. 105 sessions open one but record 0 s: a special value worth a question.

Why?

Median: half the sessions are below it. IQR (interquartile range): the middle half of the sessions. The mean is far above the median because a few sessions are very long: this is a right-skewed distribution.

The zeros are drawn apart: on a log scale 0 has no place, and mixing them in would hide everything else in one bar.
