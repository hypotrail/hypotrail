# Consent form: observed session with Hypotrail

Part of the [observation protocol](../OBSERVATION-PROTOCOL.md), §4. Read it with the participant, give them a copy, and keep the signed copy apart from the session data (it carries their name; the data carry only the pseudonym).

---

**Study.** A test of Hypotrail, an experimental tool that guides a small machine-learning study through an AI assistant (Claude Code) in the terminal.

**Purpose.** To find what is confusing or unhelpful in the tool. **We are testing the tool, not you.** There are no right or wrong ways to use it.

**What you will do.** About 90 minutes on day 1 and 25 minutes on day 2 (one or two days later): work with the assistant on a study of public bank-marketing data while thinking aloud, then answer seven short questions without the assistant, and talk with the facilitator for about ten minutes.

**Voluntary.** You can stop at any time, skip any question, and withdraw your data later by contacting the facilitator, without giving a reason and without any consequence.

**What the assistant sees.** Your messages to the assistant are sent to the model provider (Anthropic), under the terms of the account used for the session, as with any use of Claude Code. Please do not type personal or confidential information. The data in the study are public.

**What we keep, on a local computer only.** Your name is never written in these files: you are "P__".

| Item | Kept? | Your choice |
|---|---|---|
| Facilitator's notes and observation grid (pseudonymised) | Always | — |
| Your answers to the questionnaire and the interview notes (pseudonymised) | Always | — |
| The session report: commands, error codes, counts, and times, **without anything you typed** | Always | — |
| The study folder, which records the words you used to confirm decisions | Only if you agree | ☐ yes ☐ no |
