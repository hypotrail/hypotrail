- Strict optional `analysis_contract` metadata and supplement migration support.
- Opt-in `settings.reliability_profile`; omission retains the legacy state shape.
- Derived full-view/selected-summary assurance and localized cooperative-protection notes.
- Hotel leakage-exercise scope before scores; retrospective Bank full/Auto MPG contracts.
- Explicit refusal of unimplemented new-profile analytical execution; structural quality
  inspection remains supported without fitting, confirming a decision or opening the test.
- Future/group-disjoint initialization refused before dataset reads until J1 exists. This
  prevents accidentally sealing a legacy partition for a future or entity-disjoint claim.
- Independent and planted-clock synthetic fixtures; no decision imported by a contract.
- Historical WP-J case-equivalence test uses the exact two pre-J0 reconstructed versions,
  while a separate J0 test verifies that the new substantive Hotel contract triggers source
  change. `EQUIVALENT_CASE_FILES` was not widened.
- Review-profile prediction statements are bound to the declared scenario after whitespace
  normalization; guidance supplies that value. Legacy decisions remain free text.
- The declared units of the two clocks must be comparable.
- Protection-note wording is centralized in `cmd_data.NOTE` and `junior.DATA_NOTE_FR`, and
  the data card contains the notice once.
