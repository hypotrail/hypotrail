# J3 implementation and acceptance

Date: 22 October 2026. J3 was admitted only after the J1 and J2 targeted suites passed.
Legacy results retain their historical objective and baseline labels.

## Implemented scope

- One pure objective module shared by tuning, comparison, policy and final evaluation.
- Precision/recall at a fixed action rule with pooled expected counts and explicit batches.
- Row-weighted regression MAE and fold-local training-median baselines.
- Complete point/lower-bound decision truth table and qualified uncertainty descriptor.
- Conservative paired bootstrap: any undefined required replicate removes the interval.
- Objective-aware invalidation; fixed predictions are not needlessly refitted.
- Report/notebook selection surfaces explicitly suppress incoherent pooled-count one-SE and
  optimism calculations.

## Limits

Inferential bounds are qualified only for independent held-out rows and a fixed selected
configuration. Temporal/grouped dependence and batch-preserving resampling currently expose
an unavailable reason. One-SE and optimism remain available only for their legacy
equal-fold-mean setting; they are not presented as equivalence or search corrections.
