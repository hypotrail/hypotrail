# Reliability roadmap — human observation sheet

Status: **not observed**. Do not recruit or contact participants without explicit authority.
An eventual convenience sample of four to six juniors is qualitative, not a powered learning
study and not a release gate.

Record participant role (junior/reviewer), anonymous session ID, date, case/language, tested
commit, start/end time, observer prompts, unexpected events, and consent procedure. Observe one
bounded study without coaching beyond the protocol.

After completion, ask the junior to explain unaided:

1. what is predicted and what is known at prediction time;
2. why the split matches or fails to match intended use;
3. what the metric and baseline mean;
4. what gain and uncertainty do and do not establish;
5. whether a risk score proves an intervention helps;
6. what changing the selected rule requires recomputing.

Score each answer `0` incorrect/missing, `1` partly correct with a prompt, or `2` correct
without a prompt. Quote examples and record every prompt; repeating agent wording is not by
itself evidence of understanding.

The reviewer independently locates objective, protocol, chosen configuration, evidence scope,
and main limitation in the deliverables. Record time-to-find and discrepancies. Satisfaction,
learning efficacy, and chat-versus-panel comparisons require separate instruments and designs.
