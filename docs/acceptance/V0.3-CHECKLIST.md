Evidence types:

- **U** unit and **S** simulated tests (real `hypotrail` CLI processes, headless Marimo, offline Playwright, the panel's real controller on the real CLI). Paths are relative to `framework/tests/study/` unless they start with `clients/` or `framework/`. "Pass" means passed in the gate run below.

A result is the latest round of that scenario and case.
- Status: **done** (built and verified at the level stated), **partial** (built, with a named gap), **not proven** (nothing establishes it, reason given).

## Not proven

- **No human junior, no Copilot, no real VS Code.** Every agentic run answers a scripted or simulated junior; Copilot's instruction files and interactive-choice equivalent are untested; the panel and its Marimo launches are tested with Playwright and a fake `vscode` module only.
- **The smoothness targets are measured on a junior who adopts everything.** Two of the three are not met even on that shortest path; a real junior asks more.
- **`AskUserQuestion`** (R16) and the panel-related agent rules (R13, R20, R35) never ran: `claude -p` has neither.
- **Slow diagnostics on Hotel** (about 4.5 minutes idle, mostly the coefficient bootstrap) made several runs end on an interrupted execution; the engine was not changed.
- **N = 3, one re-run.** The last harness fixes (S13 without setup diagnostics, "vous" phrasing, the future marker) are not confirmed by a run.
- **Heuristic graders and `check`.** Language, refusal visibility, R32 wording and the 2×2 are detected by word lists and patterns; `check` was widened again.
