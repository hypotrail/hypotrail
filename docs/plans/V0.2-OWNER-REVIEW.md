# v0.2 owner review guide

For the owner, after the autonomous v0.2a and v0.2b runs (19 October 2026). Two parts: the decisions most worth a look, taken alone by the integrator or a work package (the full log, with every "Review?", is [V0.2-DECISIONS.md](V0.2-DECISIONS.md)), and a script for owner demo 2. What is built and what is proven: [the handoff](../handoff/IMPLEMENTATION.md) and [the v0.2 checklist](../acceptance/V0.2-CHECKLIST.md).

## Decisions to review (24 of the log's "Review?" items)

Each line: the default taken, then the alternative. The tag in brackets points into the log.

### Confirmation (R1, R3)

1. **Guided mode has no token overlap** [I2]: an explicit endorsement bound to the open proposal confirms ("oui je confirme cela"); training mode keeps concept matching. Alternative: require one shared word with the restatement in guided too.
2. **The relation and reason checks are lexical** [WP-A9 H1]: two related content words make a reply relevant, and any clause beyond the value's own words counts as a reason ("Yes, call everyone scored at 0.5 or more" passes; "oui, la pizza du client est bonne pour la campagne" would pass the relation check). Alternative: in guided, require a reason connector ("because", "parce que") as training does.
3. **Any "?" makes a reply a question** [WP-A2]; "go with that" is not an endorsement, "I adopt" is. Alternative: only a final question mark, or a broader endorsement list.
4. **Training mode asks to name every excluded variable** and a reason connector [WP-B2]. Alternative: accept a group name ("the call variables") or the count.
5. **Mode changes** [WP-B2]: raising strictness needs no words; lowering needs words naming the target mode; setting the same mode needs words. Alternative: words for every change.
6. **Guided standing delegation covers the default preprocessing** [WP-B6c, UX]; a non-default preparation still needs the junior's words. Alternative: always ask at that pause (one more confirmation, nine in all).
7. **A partial refusal is `conditional_reply` in every mode** [WP-A9 B1]; "pas pour", "not for", "but not" refuse anywhere ("ce n'est pas pour la production" is refused). Alternative: only after an agreement word.

### Final test and evaluation (R10)

8. **20 % sealed with seed 0, not configurable** [WP-A3]. Alternative: a case key (fraction, seed) for small or large data.
9. **The data card and structural EDA read development rows only**; the final test gets a schema pass/fail, and a failure refuses `init` [I3, WP-A3]. Alternative: structural inspection on all rows (the literature cited favours development rows only).
10. **Cross-validation is the drafted default, not the only scheme** [WP-B1]: a train/validation split stays accepted. Alternative: CV only in new studies.
11. **Temporal folds are contiguous blocks predicted from all other blocks**, later ones included [WP-B1]: stability across periods, not a forward backtest. Alternative: forward-chaining folds (fewer scored rows).
12. **A variant is mandatory** [WP-B1]: no "no variant" option before the choice. Alternative: allow "no variant" with a stated limit.
13. **An interrupted opening consumes the final test** [WP-B1]: the reservation marks it opened before any row is read. Alternative: allow a retry when no row was read.
14. **Rules under CV apply within each fold** (a share or capacity per fold, counts summed, bootstrap within folds); thresholds stay pooled [WP-B6c H3]. Alternative: pooled for all (simpler, but Bank full looked useless pooled: 6.8 % against 10.4 % within folds).
15. **One judged criterion**: the final test once its evaluation measured the current configuration, development evidence otherwise; `keep_baseline` against a met final criterion is a limit and a `check` warning [WP-B6c H4]. Alternative: show both judgements side by side.

### Data and cases (R6, R11)

16. **Mirrored data without a byte comparison with UCI** [I6, WP-B3]: Online Shoppers from one GitHub mirror, Bank full from two identical ones, checked against UCI's published counts. Alternative: a `prepare.py` that downloads from UCI on the owner's machine. Owner TODO either way: compare the sha256 with the UCI archive.
17. **Online Shoppers descriptions are `scenario_assumption`**, and `PageValues`, `BounceRates`, `ExitRates` are `uncertain` [WP-B3]. Alternative: read the UCI page and Sakar et al. 2019, then promote what is documented to `documented_fact`.
18. **Bank full keeps its period shift** [integrator after WP-B6b]: the final test's positive rate is 31.8 % against 7.3 % in development, the criterion fails in development and passes on the final test; taught as drift. Alternative: another order, another teaching share, or Bank full only as a generality test.
19. **`prior_exposed_rows`**: the 893 public `bank.csv` rows in the last fifth are kept out of Bank full's final test and placed in the development rows (last fold) [WP-B6a]. Alternative: drop them from the study.
20. **Teaching share 10 %, gain points at 5, 10 and 20 %, `share:p` rounding half up with at least one row** [WP-B3, WP-A9 H4]. Alternative: ask a count, as v0.1 did.

### EDA, diagnostics, and check (R8, R9)

21. **Single-feature flag thresholds** (AUC at least 0.75, or 0.15 above the median; R² at least 0.8, or 0.4 above) and **every run counts as a look**; notebook and workbench reads are not in the ledger [WP-A4, WP-A6]. Alternative: other thresholds; count notebook reads.
22. **Diagnostics are activities, not a pause**: the agent may read them and propose the rule in one message [WP-B4, integrator after WP-B6b]. Alternative: a stop of their own (one more turn).
23. **`check` was widened in v0.2b** (data card figures, criterion figures, diagnostic coefficients and bounds, group labels, calibration bins) so that agents can cite what the engine shows [WP-A8, WP-B6b]; a fabricated figure equal to some registered value still passes. Alternative: require a result id in the prose for these figures.

### Panel

24. **Opening the final test and changing the mode are chat acts**: the panel explains them and refuses W1 for a `final_test` proposal [WP-B5]. Alternative: a typed-words control in the panel (would need an ADR 0004 amendment, as W1 does today for other decisions).

## Owner demo 2 script

Goal: walk Online Shoppers in guided mode, as a junior would, in French or English, and look at the chat, the HTML report, the two notebooks, and the study panel with the flag on. About 60 to 90 minutes. Nothing here trains a business model beyond the study's own fits.

### 0. Prepare

```sh
REPO=~/source/hypotrail
STUDY=~/studies/shoppers
cd "$REPO" && git pull && uv sync --project framework --group dev
uv run --project "$REPO/framework" hypotrail init \
  --case "$REPO/use-cases/online-shoppers/case.yaml" --study "$STUDY"
cat "$STUDY/.vscode/settings.json"      # the engine's interpreter (R7)
```

If `$STUDY` already exists from an earlier try, choose a new folder (for example `~/studies/shoppers-2`): never reset an existing study. The study must stay **outside** the repository.

Check at once: `init` printed `"final_test": {"status": "sealed", "rows": 2466, ...}` and `"development_rows": 9864`.

### 1. Open VS Code with the panel

```sh
HYPOTRAIL_COMPANION=1 HYPOTRAIL_COMPANION_STAGE=v3 \
  code --extensionDevelopmentPath="$REPO/clients/vscode-companion" "$STUDY"
```

Trust the workspace, run "Hypotrail: open the study panel". Look at: the header chip "Final test sealed · 2466 rows"; the Overview opens on "Your next move" (look at the data first); no engine instruction to the assistant outside "Details". This is also the first real VS Code session: note anything for [MANUAL-TEST.md](../../clients/vscode-companion/MANUAL-TEST.md) (record it in `docs/acceptance/runs/companion-<stage>-<date>.md`).

### 2. Chat (Claude Code in the study folder)

In a VS Code terminal: `cd "$STUDY" && claude`. Guided mode is the default; do not set a mode. Say each line below in your own words; what to watch is after the arrow.

1. "Je veux faire cette étude, par où on commence ?" → step 0: the data card (row unit, column groups with meaning and availability, five rows with `Revenue` first); **development rows** and **final test** introduced as terms first, then explained; no proposal in the same message.
2. Prediction moment: "oui je confirme cela" → confirmed at the first attempt (R1). Then try a bare "ok" on the next proposal → answered by the agent, not sent to the engine.
3. Feature availability: give a misattributed reason, for example "on écarte aussi BounceRates, sa valeur peut inclure l'achat de la session" → the agent stops **before** confirming, says the reason belongs to `PageValues`, and offers (a) reformulate, (b) confirm with other words, (c) keep your words (R3). Choose (c) once to see the agent's concern in the report.
4. Split → a 5-fold stratified cross-validation, introduced with **cross-validation** and **out-of-fold predictions**. Success criterion → fixed before any model (for example "5 points de précision de plus que la référence").
5. "Continue" → exploration with intervals and the null band; the single-feature signal flags `PageValues` as a legitimacy question, not a reason to keep it; then the default preprocessing (it may be covered by your standing delegation, decision 6 above).
6. Variant → one of: a weaker or stronger regularisation, or the model without one variable group; the fold spread is read before any difference.
7. Rule → the capacity asked as a **share**, with 5, 10 and 20 % shown first and 10 % presented as the exercise's assumption (R6); give a reason. Ask "le modèle est-il bien calibré ?" → diagnostics labelled development evidence (R9).
8. "Tout est choisi : on peut ouvrir le test final ?" → nothing opened; the agent states it happens once, the result counts even if it disappoints, any later change is flagged, and asks your words with a reason. Then "oui, j'ouvre le test final maintenant, parce que mes choix sont figés" → opened in that turn, final figures first, development figures after and labelled. Then ask to open it again with the variant → refused, "once".
9. Conclusion → proportionate, with the limits; `keep_baseline` would be valid.
10. Close the chat, open a new one, type `/hypotrail-resume` → the recap quotes your latest words and names the next step.

### 3. HTML report

Open `$STUDY/reports/study.html` in a browser (offline). Look at: the verdict card first, with one judged criterion (final once opened); "The data"; 3 to 5 inline charts (target balance, rate by group with intervals, selection or gain chart); decisions with their basis ("your words", "endorsed", "from the case, not discussed", the agent's concern); evidence by scope (development, final); "What is not yet defensible" (capacity assumed for the exercise, public data, development figures used to choose); the collapsed annex. Hover a number: every figure carries a result id. Run `uv run --project "$REPO/framework" hypotrail check --study "$STUDY"` → clean.

### 4. Notebooks

```sh
uv run --project "$REPO/framework" marimo run  "$STUDY/notebooks/study_generated.py"   # read
uv run --project "$REPO/framework" marimo edit "$STUDY/notebooks/workbench.py"         # edit
```

In the generated notebook: readable pandas and scikit-learn cells that recompute the split, the explorations and the fits from the development rows and `assert` equality with the registry; the header says "Regenerated by Hypotrail at revision N; copy any cell to your workbench". In the workbench: the starter cell loads the development training rows; copy one cell and change it. Optionally open the `.py` in VS Code with the Marimo extension: the kernel should be the engine's interpreter from `.vscode/settings.json`, with no `ModuleNotFoundError: mlkit` (R7).

### 5. Study panel (flag on, stage v3)

- **Overview**: "Your next move" at every step; "Where we are" with the mode row ("Answers") and the success criterion; deliverables with **"Open in Marimo (read)"** and **"Open in Marimo (edit)"** (a named terminal opens; clicking again reuses it; nothing in the study changes) (R4).
- **Data & EDA**: the data card at the top with the dictionary and five rows (R5); the quality card; charts titled by questions with n and intervals; the look ledger; run one more per-column chart (W4).
- **Decisions**: values in words ("Select the top 10% by score"), basis chips, your words, the agent's concern; confirm one proposal from the panel with typed words (W1) and check the chat sees it.
- **Evidence**: the final test first (sealed, then opened once), the exposure ledger in words, development evidence (CV, variant) apart from final evidence, diagnostics.
- **What if?**: the share slider and the gain curve; preview a share that was not evaluated (preview only), propose one that was (W3).
- **Learn & hand off**: glossary hovers, the handoff export (W7) into a new folder.
- Switch the theme to High Contrast and narrow the panel below 600 px: buttons keep their borders; tables become cards.

### 6. Note for the review

For each step: did the junior's path feel heavy (nine confirmations), was any message written for the agent rather than for you, did any figure lack a source, and which of the 24 decisions above you would change.
