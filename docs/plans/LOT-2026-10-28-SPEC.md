## 1. Scope, non-goals, owner decisions

### 1.1 Scope

1. One structure for every step, in the panel's Now, in the path's replay of a done step and in the report's chapters: the step in one picture, what the step must establish (2–3 engine-owned questions), "to answer, look" (one chart per question with a three-part reading bound to registered values), the limit and what comes next (R61, R62, R64).
2. A new regression step **Error measure** before the success criterion: 14 steps for a regression study created after this lot (R60, option A).
3. Pictures and data for the framing steps that today show none: the prediction-moment timeline with a slider (R57), the kept and excluded variables (R58), how the sealed rows were drawn (R63), what a 5-fold cross-validation is, before any fit (R59).
4. A pause after the exploration so its charts are read before the models (R61).
5. Wording: questions that match their options (R54), "validation croisée à 5 plis", "lignes de développement", full step names (R56), "référence (prédire la moyenne)".
6. Leftovers of the R34–R53 lot: the final test's row-level observed-vs-predicted and the error by model year on the final rows; the nested-CV diagram of the ridge penalty; the What if? drawer's duplicate; equal axes on observed vs predicted (R62).

### 1.2 Non-goals

- No new panel write. The panel's writes stay **W4, W5, W7, W8** and the three Marimo launches (ADR 0004 decisions 12–14). The timeline slider, "guess first" and "replay the step" are display only: nothing is recorded, no CLI call is made.
- The panel decides nothing, records no words, composes no reading. Every reading is an engine text bound to registered values.
- No change to the generated or editable notebook beyond what an engine text change carries through on its own.
- No binary "error measure" step (§1.4). No change to What if? content beyond the de-duplication (PA-12).
- No Copilot acceptance session, no real VS Code run, no pixel baselines in this lot (the owner wants a demo first; T- tests are targeted, not full suites).
- No migration of existing studies: a study created before this lot keeps its plan (13 regression steps), its decisions and its results. Nothing in `.pilot`, `.osml`, `.local` or a study folder is reset.

## 2. The per-step structure

### 2.1 During a step (learning)

- **ST-1** Now shows, for the current step, four blocks in this order, each numbered 1–4 in a small circle as in the mockup:
  1. **The step in one picture** (« L'étape en une image »): one drawing of the concept and a caption of **≤ 30 words**, with a fold « En savoir plus » (≤ 80 words). The picture shows no target value. It may use structural values of the study (row counts, column names, fold sizes) or a fictitious example, which is then marked « exemple fictif » / "made-up example" on the drawing.
  2. **What the step must establish** (« Ce que l'étape doit établir »): one sentence « Ces réponses préparent … » naming what the step prepares, then the step's questions numbered Q1–Q3, then the chat block (« Le chat attend »): the awaited question, the engine's options as plain text, and the copy button. **≤ 60 words** for the sentence plus the questions (the chat block is not counted).
  3. **To answer, look** (« Pour répondre, regardez »): a frame line (which rows, which evidence, the look count when the target is read), then one section per question: the question repeated, its main chart open with its frame, the three-part reading (ST-3), a verdict chip only under ST-6, then the folds « Comment le lire », « Attention », and « Autres graphiques (n) » when the question has more charts.
  4. **What it does not say · next** (« Ce que ça ne dit pas · Ensuite »): the step's limit (one line) and what comes next with its why (one line).
- **ST-2** No conclusion before the evidence while learning: block 2 carries no recommendation and no reading; the engine's recommended option is shown only as the chat shows it (the options line), never as a verdict.
- **ST-3** Under every chart, three labelled parts, **about 80 words in all (hard cap 90)**:
  - « Ce qu'on voit » / "What we see": the facts, every number cited from a registered value;
  - « Ce qu'on en conclut » / "What we conclude": what it means for this step's decision, without recommending an option;
  - « Ce qu'on ne peut pas conclure » / "What we cannot conclude": the limit of this chart.
  A line under the three parts: « chiffres cités du résultat enregistré · révision N » (N = the result's registered revision), each number hoverable to its result and path.
- **ST-4** « Attention » holds only reading traps (how the chart can mislead: binned means hide spread, a log axis, small groups), never the limit (which is ST-3's third part).
- **ST-5** Workshop option « Deviner d'abord » / "Guess first" (panel "more" menu; a per-viewer convenience in `vscode.setState`, key `guess_first`, default off): the three-part readings of block 3 are hidden behind a button « Voir ce qu'on lit » per section; nothing is recorded, no CLI call. The report has no such option.
- **ST-6** A verdict chip (« atteint » / « non atteint », « seuil dépassé ») appears only when the engine registers a verdict with its bound (`question.verdict` non-null, EN-7): the success criterion of a configuration, a quality rule against its threshold. Never on a distribution, a correlation or a diagnostic shape.

### 2.2 After a step (reviewing)

- **ST-7** Review form, used by the path's detail of a done step (R55), the automatic-mode review and the report (RE-): first the decision taken (words, basis, who decided: « vos mots », « délégué », « automatique »), then the answers: each question with its « Ce qu'on voit » sentence and a « Voir le graphique » toggle that opens, in place, the chart and the full three-part reading; then the step's limit; then « Rejouer l'étape telle qu'elle était » / "Replay the step".
- **ST-8** "Replay the step" restores the four blocks of ST-1 read only, with block 2's chat block replaced by the decision taken. It shows the results the step rested on: current ones, or, when stale, the same results marked by ST-11.
- **ST-9** A step not reached shows its full name and nothing else (no picture, no questions).

### 2.3 Special cases

- **ST-10** A step without a chart keeps block 1; block 3 reads « Pas de graphique pour ce point » followed by the engine's reason (`no_chart`). A step with a single question drops the Q numbering in blocks 2 and 3.
- **ST-11** A question whose result is stale (`status: "stale"`): the chart is drawn greyed with a chip « Périmé : ne correspond plus à l'étude » and only « Ce qu'on voit » is shown while learning; in review, all three parts are shown under the same chip. A question whose result is not yet computed (`status: "pending"`) shows « Calcul en cours, lancé depuis le chat. Le résultat s'affichera ici ; rien à répondre maintenant. » (R43 wording) or, when nothing is running, « Pas encore calculé ».
- **ST-12** At 420 px (and 320 px) every block is readable without horizontal scroll: charts scale to the panel width, the correlation matrix uses the engine's short column names (EN-14), axis labels wrap, no text is cut. 600 px is the comfortable width.
- **ST-13** Length limits are engine-enforced for engine texts (EN-8) and panel-enforced for nothing: the panel never truncates an engine text.

### 3.2 Readings for the mockup's three steps (normative examples, FR)

These fix tone and length; numbers are placeholders filled from registered values (EN-5).

- `explore.target` — vu: « mpg va de {min} à {max}, médiane {median}, sur {n} voitures. » conclu: « La plupart des voitures sont entre {q10} et {q90} mpg{tail_clause}. Une erreur de quelques mpg pèse plus sur une voiture peu sobre que sur une voiture sobre. » ne peut pas: « Ce graphique ne dit rien du lien entre mpg et les autres colonnes, ni des voitures hors de {period}. »
- `variants.period` — vu: « Le biais passe de {first_bias} mpg ({first_period}) à {last_bias} ({last_period}). » conclu (variant `drift`, when \|last − first\| ≥ the engine's drift bound): « Le modèle se trompe dans un sens qui change avec les années : une limite à écrire dans la conclusion. » ne peut pas: « Avec {k} blocs, c'est un signal, pas une preuve ; rien ne dit comment le modèle se comporterait après {last_year}. »
- `conclusion.criterion` — vu: « Réduction de {gain} [{low} ; {high}], au-dessus du minimum de {min_share}. » conclu (variant `met_low`): « Le critère fixé avant tout modèle est atteint, même au bas de l'intervalle. » ne peut pas: « Atteindre le minimum ne dit pas que le modèle suffit pour tout usage : c'est une question métier. »

Template variants (`met_low`, `met_point`, `not_met`, `drift`, `stable`, `tail`, `no_tail` …) are chosen by `step_cards.py` from registered values with a rule in code; the content file holds only text.

### 3.3 Binary differences (brief)

No step 5. Step 6 `criterion.bar` names the ranking measure. Step 9: `explore.target` → `eda.target_rate` (`rate_bar`), `explore.features` → `eda.rate_by:<col>`. Step 10 `models.beat` → `fit.baseline`, `fit.logistic` (AUC, precision at the rule), `models.where` → `diag.binary` (gain curve). Step 11 `variants.shape` → `diag.binary` (calibration, segments). An extra step **Rule / Règle** (`policy`): `rule.cost`: What does each rule select, gain and cost? → `policy.evaluation` → `bars`; `rule.share`: How does precision fall as the share grows? → `diag.binary` curve. Step 14 uses the final precision at the rule. The strata picture (R63) shows the class strata as "same share sealed in each class" **without counts** (counts per class would reveal the outcome rate before any look): `strata_counts` is `null` for a binary study and the reading says why.

## 4. Engine requirements

Owner: package E (§7). All digest keys are **additive** (CLI-CONTRACT §17 rule): a client that ignores them still works; `status --summary` without `--digest` stays byte-for-byte unchanged.

### 4.1 Questions, readings, texts

- **EN-1 Question registry.** New module `framework/mlkit/study/step_cards.py` with `QUESTIONS`: for each question id of §3 (and §3.3): `step`, `tasks` (`regression`, `binary` or both), `source` (`{"kind": "result"|"digest"|"case"|"illustration"|"decision", "slot"|"key"}`), `chart`, `other_charts`, `frame`, `when` (a predicate on the view: e.g. `choice.penalty` only with a tuned variant), and the filler function that returns placeholder values with their citations. Question order per step is the registry order.
- **EN-2 Content file.** New file `framework/mlkit/study/content/step_cards.yaml`, schema `hypotrail.content.step_cards/1`, owned by E (the only content file E owns). Per step: `name`, `short`, `picture.caption`, `picture.more`, `prepares`, `limit` (may reuse `junior.STEP_LIMITS`), `next`, `no_chart`; per question: `text`, `reading.<variant>.{see, conclude, cannot}`, `how`, `trap`; each a `{en, fr}` pair. **No digit** in any text (placeholders `{name}` only); a placeholder present in `en` is present in `fr` and vice versa.
- **EN-3 Lint.** `framework/tests/study/test_content_lint.py` refuses: any `[0-9]` in a text of `content/step_cards.yaml` and of `content/lesson.yaml`; a placeholder not produced by the question's filler (checked statically against the filler's declared names); a missing `en` or `fr`; « moyenne de référence » or « moyenne d'entraînement » in any FR text of `content/` and of engine `.py` strings (EN-21).
- **EN-4 Card builder.** `step_cards.build(study, built, lang) -> dict` returns the `step_cards` object of §7.2 for every plan step: `state` from the plan (`current`, `done`, `upcoming`), the picture, the questions whose `when` holds, each with its `status` (`answered` when its result is current with integrity ok, `stale`, `pending`, `no_chart`), `result_id`, `result_revision`, `reading`, `cites`, `verdict`, `chart_data` where §7.2 says so. The same function feeds the digest (EN-19) and the report (RE-1).
- **EN-5 Readings bound to registered values.** Every number in a filled reading is formatted by `cite.format_value` from a value at a path of a registered result, a digest key computed from registered results (`quality_verdicts`, `criterion_configurations`, `data_structure`, `ledger`), the partition record, or an engine constant of an illustration (EN-10). Each placeholder yields a `cites[]` entry `{name, result_id, path, value, text}` (`result_id: null` and `path: "engine:<CONSTANT>"` for a constant). A stale result still fills its reading (ST-11); a missing result yields `reading: null`.
- **EN-6 Binary readings.** Shared steps (1–4, 6–9, 13) have full three-part readings for binary studies. For binary-only questions (`models.*`, `variants.*`, `choice.*`, `rule.*`, `conclusion.*` on binary results) `see` is filled from values and `conclude`/`cannot` may be static E0 texts in this lot.
- **EN-7 Verdicts.** `verdict` is non-null only for: `quality.*` rules (`{label, met: exceeds, value, bound: threshold, result_id, path}` from `quality_verdicts.columns[].checks[]`), `choice.best`/`choice.simplest` per configuration (`criterion_configurations`), `conclusion.criterion` (final assess). Labels: « seuil dépassé » / « sous le seuil », « atteint » / « non atteint ».
- **EN-8 Length.** `step_cards.build` measures every filled text: caption ≤ 30 words, `prepares` + questions ≤ 60, each reading ≤ 90 (target 80), `limit` and `next` ≤ 30 each. A longer text is a bug: the build raises in tests (`HYPOTRAIL_STRICT_TEXTS=1`) and logs otherwise; it never truncates.

### 4.2 The new step and the plan

- **EN-9 Decision `error_metric`.** A regression study created by `init` after this lot holds a decision `error_metric` (value `{"metric": "mae"}` or `{"metric": "rmse"}`), placed before `success_criterion`: a new decision set `M1_REGRESSION_DECISION_IDS` (B1 regression + `error_metric`), read from the state like the WP-A4 and WP-B1 sets (`decision_ids` reads which set a state holds; never migrated). `steps()` inserts `ERROR_METRIC_STEP` (pause 2, moment « Cadrer le changement », title "Choose how the error is measured") before `success_criterion` when the state holds the decision. `success_criterion` `depends_on` it: changing the measure sets the criterion to `reconfirm_required`. The criterion's value keeps its form (`mae_reduction` / `rmse_reduction`, CLI-CONTRACT §17.6); with `error_metric` confirmed, a criterion whose metric differs is refused `invalid_value` with `details.error_metric`, and the criterion draft takes the confirmed measure. A study without the decision keeps choosing the measure inside the criterion (R46 path) and its 13 steps.
- **EN-10 Draft and illustration.** The `error_metric` draft recommends `mae` with its why (« chaque erreur pèse pareil, et elle se lit en mpg »), RMSE as the alternative (« une grosse erreur pèse beaucoup plus : à choisir si un gros raté coûte bien plus que plusieurs petits »); question (R54 form): « Quelle mesure de l'erreur retient-on : l'erreur absolue moyenne (MAE) ? » with options adopt / change / explain. Constants in `step_cards.py`: `METRIC_EXAMPLE_ERRORS = (1, 1, 1, 1, 6)`; the card's `chart_data` gives `errors`, `mae` (2.0), `squares` (1, 1, 1, 1, 36), `mean_square` (8.0), `rmse` (√8, cited as 2.83), `unit` (the case's target unit). When the target unit is `mpg`, `unit_note` gives « {x} mpg ≈ {y} L/100 km » at the illustrative value `UNIT_NOTE_MPG = 25` (y = 235.215 / x); no study value is used.
- **EN-11 Delegation, automation.** `error_metric` joins `EXPRESS_STANDING_DELEGATION_DECISIONS`; the automatic mode records `mae` with basis `automatic` (it stays in the review queue like any automatic decision). It reads no data and records no exposure.
