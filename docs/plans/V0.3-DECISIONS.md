## WP-A Engine correctness

- A1 One tracer: the tracing core moved from `cmd_check.py` to `mlkit/study/numtrace.py` (re-exported by `cmd_check`); `check --text`, the study check of the conclusion and `decide propose` build the same pool (`numtrace.study_pool`), `propose` only adds the proposed value as a literal (never for the conclusion).
- A2 L4 ("top250" is a number) now applies in `check` too, so the two commands agree; a single letter before digits (`x2`) and hexadecimal digests stay identifiers. Review?
- A3 A bare "points" is percentage points: it matches a registered difference times 100 (`deltas`, `difference`, a `gap`, a `gain`), else a value already in points (`gain_points`, the criterion) or a recorded literal; a share written in points stays untraceable (D4 kept).
- A4 Scope binding: a sentence that names a slot with a dot or a result id prefix (6+ hex characters) binds to that result; `check --result` (new, repeatable) binds a whole draft; a named group label is matched first and excludes other groups. Common words ("yes", "other", "unknown", "may") are never group names. Review?
- A5 A diagnostic's segment figures are citable only when the sentence names the group (coincidence otherwise too likely); policy-evaluation `by_fold` figures became internal like fits' `per_fold`.
- A6 A figure bound to a metric word never traces to a setting, nor a percentage of a metric to a data-card cell or final-test count (the bootstrap level 0.95 had made "95% precision" traceable once propose shared check's pool). A bare "points" is the target unit, not percentage points, when the study's unit is "points" (Diabetes).
- A7 The final-test confirmation reports the revision after its opening run; replayed receipts keep their recorded revision.
- A8 R34: the diagnostics' currency is keyed on the rule in their registry `params.rule`; the report's rule-source labels were reworded to stay true after an adoption ("the teaching scenario's share, before any rule was proposed").
