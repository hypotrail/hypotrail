# Plan: the one-page study panel (ADR 0004 decision 13)

Status: panel side done (P-0 to P-6 and P-8, on hand-built P1 fixtures; P-7: the tabbed baselines deleted, the one-page set generated locally and waiting for the owner's review, not committed); engine side (E-1 to E-8) in progress separately. Specification: [COMPANION-ADVANCED.md](../specs/COMPANION-ADVANCED.md) (§3.2, §4, §6, §10, §13, §16, §18, Appendix A). Engine contract: [CLI-CONTRACT.md §17](../specs/CLI-CONTRACT.md) (contract P1). Approved mockup: [panel-synthesis-fr.html](mockups/panel-synthesis-fr.html).

Rules for every package:

- The writes stay exactly W4, W5 and W7, plus the three Marimo launches; `scripts/check_layout.py` must stay clean after every package.
- The panel stays behind the flag, off by default, stage `v0` by default; the chat, the report and the notebook stay complete without it.
- Tests use temporary studies and the captured fixtures; no existing study state is reset.
- No assertion is weakened to pass. A test of the tabbed panel that no longer applies is **rewritten** against its new requirement id (spec §18) or **deleted with its requirement** when §18 says "withdrawn", in the same commit, and the commit says which.
- **Pixel baselines** (`clients/vscode-companion/test/baselines/*.png`) are regenerated by P-7 with `--update-baselines` into the working tree and **not committed until the owner has reviewed them** from the diff images in `clients/vscode-companion/.local/baseline-diff/`. Until that review the 108 tabbed baselines are deleted in the integration commit and the pixel tests skip with the message "one-page baselines awaiting owner review"; the structural gate (`test_webview_gate.py`) must pass.
- `scripts/companion_coverage.py` gets the new prefixes (`OP`, `PG`, `TODO`, `SINCE`, `NOW`, `PATH`, `TOOL`, `NOT`, `HON`, `SAMPLE`, `NAV`, `FOLD`, `FOCUS`, `THEME`, `LANG`, `NAR`) in P-0, with the tests that name them landing in the same package as their code; `test_requirement_coverage.py` and the checklist block of `docs/acceptance/V0.1-CHECKLIST.md` are regenerated at integration.

Test kinds: **unit** (Node `node --test`, or pytest on engine modules), **real CLI** (pytest that runs the `hypotrail` CLI on temporary studies built from the Bank, Auto MPG and Hotel cases, or the companion's `realstudy.js`), **simulated browser** (Playwright on `lib/html.js` output with captured payloads, `test_webview_*.py`). None of them is a real VS Code session or a Copilot acceptance session; those stay on the manual checklist (`clients/vscode-companion/MANUAL-TEST.md`).
