## L1 — Confirmation modes and reason check (R1, R3)

- Study `mode` in state: `guided` (default), `training`, `express`. Set by `init --mode`; changed by `hypotrail mode set` with the user's verbatim words (an agent cannot lower it alone). Changing mode never invalidates past confirmations.
- `basis` gains `endorsed` (explicit endorsement) and `restated` (paraphrase); existing `delegated` unchanged.
- Endorsement detector FR/EN ("je confirme", "je valide", "d'accord pour moi", "ça me va", "yes, I confirm"...). Still refused: bare "ok/oui/yes/d'accord", conditionals ("oui mais", "d'accord sauf"), questions, negations. An endorsement is accepted only against the single open proposal named by `--proposal` at the current revision.
- Guided hybrid rule: endorsement accepted for prediction_time, feature_availability and split; policy and conclusion need one reason.
- Training: paraphrase required, matched on concepts (term + FR/EN synonyms from the glossary), plus a reason for policy and conclusion.
- Express: guided + standing delegation extended to framing decisions; policy and conclusion never delegable (R1 of v0.1 unchanged).
- Language: proposal values stay structured; restatements gain per-language labels; the agent shows the label in the conversation language.
- R3: `decide confirm --agent-concern "<text>"` recorded and rendered ("Point raised by the agent … kept by the user"); `decide note --on <decision>` attaches a later correction without reconfirming; mechanical contradiction check for feature_availability (column named with exclude/keep against the value) shipped as a **warning**.
- Acceptance: the owner's exact demo sentences ("oui je confirme cela", the contact/campaign reason) become tests; mode matrix tests; HTML/notebook show `basis`, mode and concerns.

## L2 — Data card, step 0, wider EDA (R2, R5, R8)

- `hypotrail data` (read-only, unlogged): observation unit, rows, columns, target labels; column dictionary grouped by family (type, unit, description, availability + status + source, special values, missing, distinct, 3 examples); 5 fixed rows including the target. No target rate or distributions before the split.
- Step 0 "discover the data": `next_action: show_data_card` while no proposal exists; no confirmation.
- Case contract (additive): optional `group` and `labels.fr` per variable in `case-supplement.yaml`; filled for Bank and Auto MPG.
- EDA catalog, each analysis tied to the question it answers: quality (missing overview and patterns, duplicates, cardinality, ranges/outliers) on all rows as structural inspection; per-column distributions, target by category / numeric bins, Spearman and Cramér's V with collinearity flag, and single-feature signal (cross-validated AUC or R² per feature, flagged as a leakage detector) on training rows only. Visible count of analyses viewed.
- Render: "The data" section in HTML after the objective; a notebook cell; registered results for every figure.

## L3 — Sealed holdout, CV selection, diagnostics (R10, R9)

- Split decision becomes development/holdout (default 80/20 stratified; temporal when the case declares an order). Selection by 5-fold CV on development rows; policy evaluation on out-of-fold predictions.
- Seal enforced by the engine: holdout rows excluded from every read (EDA, fit, diagnostics, data card sample) until opened.
- Opening: a human decision (`final_test`) requiring confirmed model and policy and the user's words; recorded in the exposure ledger. Any later change to model or policy is marked "after the final test" in every surface.
- Diagnostics (registered results): binary — confusion matrix at the adopted rule, gain/lift, ROC, precision–recall, calibration, metrics by segment, coefficients with intervals, example false positives/negatives; regression — residuals vs predicted, residual distribution and QQ, residuals by feature/segment, predicted vs observed. On out-of-fold predictions before opening, on the holdout after.
- Migration: existing studies keep their two-way split and are labelled "no final test"; no reset of existing state.
